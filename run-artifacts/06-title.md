## Titles

1. **What the Room Remembers** — PREFERRED. Captures the poem's core move: absence made present through furniture, dust and light. Quietly formal, suits blank verse, never overstates grief.
2. **The Name Still in the Room** — Echoes stanza 1's final line ("nothing but your name still in the room"); haunting and concrete, though slightly more explicit than the preferred option.
3. **A Hollow of Your Heat** — Drawn from stanza 2's opening image; intimate, but leans harder on the "you" than the rest of the poem's restraint.
4. **After Departure** — Plain and direct, frames the whole arc; risks stating the subject the poem prefers to imply.
5. **Late Light, Empty Chair** — Lifts two palette images into a title; pictorial, but reads more like a list than the poem's own voice.

### Subtitle idea

*on the room you left* — a small italic subtitle that classifies the poem and echoes the epigraph's parting theme without repeating any line.
